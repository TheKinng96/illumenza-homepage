{%- comment -%} Filter bar. Reaching an app's section used to take two clicks — /blog/ to /blog/points/ to /blog/points/missions/. These chips do it in one. The app chips are real links to pages that exist, so they still work with JavaScript off; blog-filter.js intercepts the click and filters in place. The search box and the section chips are hidden until that script runs, because neither has a no-JS equivalent, and a search box that does nothing is worse than no search box. Section keys are shared across apps — `appearance` belongs to both Points and Coupon — so a section chip alone is ambiguous. That is why picking an app clears the section, and why chips with nothing under the current app hide themselves. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign points_posts = site.posts | where: "app", "points" -%} {%- assign coupon_posts = site.posts | where: "app", "coupon" -%}
